How categories count in the dashboards

Income categories count as income. Investment categories count as invested instead of spent. Every other category is spending, netted per category: money coming back (a refund, or someone paying you back) reduces that category instead of counting as income.

CategoryIncomeInvestment

Display currency

Dashboard and Monthly totals are shown in this currency; other currencies are converted at the ECB reference rate of each transaction's date. You can also switch it from those pages.
