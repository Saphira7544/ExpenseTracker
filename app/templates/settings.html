How categories count in the dashboards

Income categories count as income. Investment categories count as invested instead of spent. Ignored categories are left out of every total: use them for money moving between your own accounts or currencies (e.g. Internal). Every other category is spending, netted per category: money coming back (a refund, or someone paying you back) reduces that category instead of counting as income. Occasional categories are spending too, but not part of a normal month (e.g. Travel): the Dashboard shows them apart from your regular expenses, so you can see what you save in a normal month and how much they take out of it.

AI categorization

Transactions your rules don't match are sent to OpenAI to suggest a category (only their descriptions). Once saved, your key is stored encrypted and can't be seen or copied here: to change it, paste a new one.

Display currency

Dashboard and Monthly totals are shown in this currency; other currencies are converted at the ECB reference rate of each transaction's date. You can also switch it from those pages.
